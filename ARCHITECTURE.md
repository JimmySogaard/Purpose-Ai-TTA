---

**System Design Principles:**
1. **Modularity**: Each component is independent and reusable
2. **Extensibility**: Easy to add new AI providers or features
3. **Security**: Privacy and encryption by default
4. **Transparency**: All decisions and allocations tracked
5. **Community**: 10% always goes back to the people
6. **Purpose**: Every action aligned with meaningful outcomes

**Power to Folket!** 🌟
